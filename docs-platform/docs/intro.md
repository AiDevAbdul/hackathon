This platform provides comprehensive educational content on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics, combining traditional textbook learning with modern AI-powered assistance, personalization, and multilingual support.

- **Comprehensive Content**: Detailed textbook content covering all aspects of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics
- **AI-Powered Assistance**: RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot for interactive Q&A
- **Personalization**: Content adapted to your software and hardware background

- **Modern Pedagogy**: Fun fact cards and engagement elements throughout
- **Theming Options**: Light, dark, and system themes for comfortable reading
