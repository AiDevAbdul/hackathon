## Getting Started

### Platform Overview
The Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook Platform is designed to provide an interactive and personalized learning experience focused on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. The platform includes:

- Comprehensive textbook content with modern pedagogical approaches
- Interactive RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot for Q&A
- Personalized content based on your software and hardware background
- Multilingual support (Urdu translation available)
- Fun fact cards to enhance learning
- Light/dark/system theme support

### System Requirements
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection
- JavaScript enabled

## Authentication & Account Management

### Creating an Account
1. Navigate to the registration page
2. Fill in your email, password, and name
3. Optionally provide your software and hardware background for personalization
4. Click "Register"
5. Check your email for a verification link (if required)

### Logging In
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. You'll be redirected to your personalized dashboard

### Account Settings
- Update your profile information
- Change your password
- Manage your personalization preferences
- Update your theme preferences

## Learning with the RAG Chatbot

### How to Use the Chatbot
1. Navigate to any textbook content page
2. Locate the RAG chatbot interface
3. Type your question related to the content
4. The chatbot will provide answers based on the textbook material
5. Review the sources cited in the response

### Best Practices
- Ask specific questions related to the textbook content
- Use the chatbot to clarify complex concepts
- Review the cited sources for additional context
- The chatbot's confidence score indicates answer reliability

### What the Chatbot Can Do
- Answer questions about Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics
- Explain complex concepts in simpler terms
- Provide examples and applications
- Reference specific textbook sections

## Personalization Features

### Setting Your Background
1. Go to your profile or preferences page
2. Set your software background (e.g., "Software Engineer", "Programmer")
3. Set your hardware background (e.g., "Robotics Enthusiast", "Electronics Engineer")
4. Save your preferences

### How Personalization Works
The platform adapts content based on your background:
- **Software-focused background**: More programming examples and code
- **Hardware-focused background**: More physical implementation and circuit details
- **Mixed background**: Balanced approach with both perspectives
- **Beginner background**: More foundational explanations and step-by-step guides

### Updating Preferences
You can update your preferences at any time, and the content adaptation will be applied to new content views.

## Translation Features

### Using Urdu Translation
1. Navigate to any textbook content page
2. Look for the translation toggle (Urdu flag or "Ur" icon)
3. Click to translate the content to Urdu
4. The translated content will appear on the page
5. Click again to switch back to English

### Translation Quality
- Translations are generated using AI technology
- Quality validation ensures accuracy
- If you notice issues with translations, please report them through feedback

### Translation Progress
- For longer content, you'll see a progress indicator
- Translation typically completes within seconds
- Translated content is cached for faster access next time

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems
- **Issue**: Can't log in
- **Solution**:
  - Verify your email and password are correct
  - Check if caps lock is on
  - Reset your password if needed
  - Clear your browser cache and cookies

#### Chatbot Not Responding
- **Issue**: RAG chatbot doesn't respond
- **Solution**:
  - Check your internet connection
  - Ensure you're asking questions related to the content
  - Try rephrasing your question
  - The system may be experiencing high load, wait and try again

#### Translation Issues
- **Issue**: Translation takes too long or fails
- **Solution**:
  - Check your internet connection
  - Some content may not be available in Urdu yet
  - Try refreshing the page

#### Content Not Loading
- **Issue**: Textbook content doesn't load
- **Solution**:
  - Refresh the page
  - Clear your browser cache
  - Try a different browser
  - Check your internet connection
